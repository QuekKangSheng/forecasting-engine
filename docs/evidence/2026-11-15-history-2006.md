# The history from 31 Aug 2006, 15 Nov 2026

Phase 3 of the 15 Nov plan. Produced by `docs/evidence/2026-11-15-history-2006.py` from the 11 Bloomberg exports Toby extracted from 31 Aug 2006 (Bloomberg access has since ended, so this is the full history available). The script reads them through the functions the Data page calls (`bloomberg_xlsx.read_export`, the per-file schema check, `bloomberg_csv.merge`, `drop_empty_columns`) rather than by clicking through the page, and fits each model through the functions the Models page's runners call, at the page's defaults. The Sep 2026 upload it compares against is the repo's `data/last_commit.pkl`. Run on `feature-claude-history-2006` after the bond research model (#40). Every file's SHA-256 is listed below, so the figures can be reproduced.

Only the research models' scores are reported here. Every other model's figures on this data belong to the single final rerun after the optimiser fixes (Phase 5); here only their runtime is.

## Summary

- **Coverage.** Every file starts on 31 Aug 2006. No target and no research-model input misses more than 0.4% of the days both targets are priced in any calendar year, so the stop rule (starts after 31 Aug 2006, or a year more than 5% missing) is not triggered, and no signal is missing from any fold.
- **Start dates.** No rule changed, and every start date moved earlier as expected, confirmed from the code below. The first test windows open on 1 Oct 2008 (equity) and 3 Oct 2008 (bond), slightly later than the plan's "about Sep 2008": the tuning period ends on 2 and 4 Sep 2008, and the 20-row embargo follows it. The forecast trust can first open on 4 Nov 2009. The portfolio backtest has 226 rebalances from 6 Oct 2008.
- **Pre-2016 hold-out.** Both research models score at about zero on the windows their research never saw, and fall well short of the gate on the whole history:
  - equity: Signal Rank IC −0.032, t −0.30 over 97 windows. Over the whole period it is +0.071, t 1.10, against +0.237, t 2.85 on the Sep 2026 upload;
  - bond: −0.004, t −0.05 over 96 windows. Over the whole period it is +0.066, t 0.99, against +0.092, t 0.90.

  Reported as they came out; neither model was changed because of them.
- **Runtime.** Locally, each model's fit takes about twice as long as on the Sep 2026 upload (1.5 to 2.5 times; the slow ones, the derived polynomial and machine learning, 1.9 to 2.2 times). One Run of every model on a tab, one after another, takes about 13 minutes for equity (6 before) and 11 for bond (5 before). Nothing ran out of time or memory locally. The hosted app is checked separately by the team.
